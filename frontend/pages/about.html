About Us

Welcome to Bunny & Bloom, a charming family café where warmth, comfort, and sweetness meet. Our café was inspired by our family’s love for bunnies and the joy of growing flowers, which is reflected in every corner of our space. At Bunny & Bloom, we specialize in hot drinks and sweet treats, offering a variety of freshly brewed coffees, aromatic teas, and comforting beverages to brighten your day.
Alongside our drinks, we serve handcrafted pastries and desserts, perfect for sharing with friends or enjoying in a quiet moment for yourself. The café is designed to be a welcoming space for families and friends, where everyone can relax, unwind, and savor the simple pleasures of life.
Every detail, from the cozy décor to the carefully curated menu, is crafted with love and care, reflecting the homely spirit that inspired our café. Whether you’re stopping by for a quick coffee, a sweet treat, or simply to enjoy the cozy atmosphere, Bunny & Bloom promises a delightful experience for all who visit.
